## Problem

Reading a thinker is a monologue. The interesting part — them arguing with each other, and with you — never happens.

## My approach

Distill 26 thinkers into data-only personas (six-layer framework plus each one's own body of work), then orchestrate them with a bid auction: after every message, everyone scores how much they want to speak; the highest bid takes the mic.

## Outcome

v1 shipped — a working salon where Nietzsche rebuts Zhuangzi and you can interject anytime, at pennies per discussion.
