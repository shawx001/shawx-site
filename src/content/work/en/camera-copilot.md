## Problem

Everyone has taste; not everyone has technique. The gap between the photo you see and the photo you take is technique — position, framing, light, settings.

## My approach

Start from the photos you love. The copilot learns your aesthetic, reads the scene in real time, and walks you to the shot: where to stand, how to frame, what to dial in — on phones, pro bodies and drones.
